Decisions: ADR-0016 and ADR-0017 accepted. Roadmap 0024 and 0025 updated. Studies: the "Boot run results" of

### What went wrong

The shell tool refused several compound commands inside the worktree, so the runs went through small scratch scripts under
`/tmp`. After the profile change I ran the old `vmr` binary once, which links the generator, and saw the old failure again; the
tool has to be rebuilt after a generator change. The documented path of the signed runner,
`release/ContainerRunner`, is a link to `out/Products/Release/ContainerRunner`; both work.

### Cleanup

Removed: the scratch directories `/tmp/vmr-agent-run` and `/tmp/vmr-agent-container`, the download directory
`/tmp/vmr-agent-dl` (the ISO, its signature and checksum, the key, the extracted kernel files and the logs), the keyring
`/tmp/vmr-agent-dl/gnupg`, `~/Library/Containers/com.example.VMRunner.VMHelper`, `~/Library/Caches/ContainerRunner`, and the
worktree's `build/sandbox`. No guest, helper or runner process was left; the processes were each started in the foreground
with a timeout. The worktree's own build directories (`.build`, `.build-packages`) remain until the worktree is removed.

### Not verified

The user-mode stack inside a helper or a container; the `custom` sandbox level; the Kata 3.26.0 kernel; boot time spread over
repeated runs; guests longer than 40 s; whether anything was visible on screen during the runs (it was not watched).
