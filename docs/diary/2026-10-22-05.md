### Decisions

Host information goes over a read-only share, not vsock, so no guest agent is needed. The kill switch uses the VPN rule
the policy already stored and applies with NAT too. The kill switch and the guest-identity settings stay out of the
security profiles. The policy is read from the app's own domain, also under `VMRUNNER_DATA_DIR`. The in-guest VPN probe
was not built: it opens a guest-to-host channel and needs an ADR first.

### Not verified

Taking the attachment off a running guest's network card and putting it back; the VPN rule against a real VPN and the
metered switch on a hotspot; the share mounted in a Linux and a macOS guest; the battery figure against the menu bar on a
laptop; a Linux guest on NVMe and the serial seen in it; a macOS guest started with a new identifier; the vendor addresses
on NAT; a real configuration profile. The three vendor prefixes match the public IEEE registry; no address with one of them
was tried on a guest.
