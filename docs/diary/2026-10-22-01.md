### What went wrong

Three study agents and a comparison agent ran out of turns without a report and were resumed with a call budget. Two agents
queued their gate runs through a capacity tool, which made the work look stalled. ADR numbers collided twice because branches
picked "the next free number" independently; both were renumbered on merge. My own Phase 0 comment edit put a double hyphen
into an XML comment the file itself forbids. Worktree removal needed two passes because of build directories. A display agent ran broad `pkill` patterns that may have interrupted other agents' test runs, and one TSan run aborted once without reproducing. My docs check was skipped once because another gate held the lock; I ran it again afterwards. The early commits of the container branch still carry "spike" in their subjects (history is not rewritten; the tree was renamed). A merged worktree was left on disk for a while until I noticed.

### Cleanup

Every merged worktree and its branch was removed after its merge; the aemi worktrees stay until the owner has reviewed them. The practice is now in
CONTRIBUTING, "Worktrees and cleanup".

### Not verified

Real guests (roadmaps 0030, 0037 to 0040: no latency number, boot text, resize or pause behaviour was seen on a guest), a guest booting under either sandbox engine, the container runner's run, the workflows on GitHub
(`actionlint` and `zizmor` never ran), the approval alert and the suspend sequence in the UI, every performance estimate in the
studies, and the reproduction of the ten suspected test-support defects.
