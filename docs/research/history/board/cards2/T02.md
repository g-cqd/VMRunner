## Why

ADR-NNNN (T01) decides the root-only layout. This card does it, with no behaviour change:

- `Sources/VirtioGPU`: every file of the former `VirtioGPURunnerCore` except `RunnerArguments.swift`. `public` becomes `package` (ADR-0047). It imports nothing at all. Target `VirtioGPU`, tests `VirtioGPUTests` (`@testable import VirtioGPU`).
- `Sources/VirtioGPURunnerCore`: `RunnerArguments.swift` alone (ADR-0042 item 3), tests `VirtioGPURunnerTests`.
- `Sources/VirtioGPURunner`: the executable, linking `VirtioGPU` and `VirtioGPURunnerCore`; its files that use the library import `VirtioGPU` (and `main.swift` and `MachineBuilder.swift` also keep `VirtioGPURunnerCore`).
- `Packages/VirtioGPU` is gone with its manifest and `Package.resolved`; `.vmr.json` (`packages`, `lintPaths`) and `.swiftlint.yml` (`included`) drop it. `Sources` and `Tests` are already linted, so the new directories are covered.
- **Layering tests, rewritten on purpose:** `ModuleBoundaryTests.theVirtioGPUPackageStaysOutOfTheRootPackageAndTheApp` (it kept the study out of the root, which is now wrong) is replaced by three tests: the library depends on no target and no package and its sources import nothing (not even Foundation); the runner links the library and its command-line parser only, and the parser depends on nothing; the app reaches neither the runner nor its parser (through any chain of targets), imports neither, and `AppBundler` builds the app's product alone. The parameterised no-UI-or-Virtualization test now points at `Sources/VirtioGPU` and `Sources/VirtioGPURunnerCore`. `PackageLayoutTests.theRootPackageDoesNotKnowTheNestedOnes` keeps its intent for the one remaining nested package (Containers) and gains a loop: the root manifest names no nested package's path. `DependencyPolicyTests.repositoryLibraries` is **unchanged**: the app does not link `VirtioGPU` yet (T05 adds it).
