## Why

A few setting rows had no trailing "?" button, so their control sat about 30 pt to the right of the rows around it. This card
gives every setting row one, in a single trailing column.

`settingHelp(_:tooltip:)` is added in `Sources/VMRunner/Views/Help/HelpButton.swift`; `settingHelp(_:)` calls it with the topic's summary
(no copy of the body). It is applied to:

- Audio: both toggles. Microphone keeps its own tooltip.
- First-boot "Create account automatically".
- Disk serial number.
- The filtered-network deny-action picker and reset toggle; they keep their own tooltips.

The Linux display caption loses its "?". The Display header's "?" now opens `linuxBootText` for Linux guests and `resolution` for macOS
guests; the Resolution picker keeps its own "?".
